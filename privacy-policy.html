Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how images-resizer.com ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use our website and tools.

1. Your images never leave your device

All image processing on this site — resizing, compressing, cropping and converting — is performed locally in your web browser using JavaScript. Your images are never uploaded to, stored on, or transmitted through our servers. We have no access to the photos you edit.

2. Information we collect

We do not require accounts and do not collect names, email addresses or other personal details when you use the tools. Like most websites, we may collect standard, non-identifying technical information such as browser type, device type, pages visited and approximate (country-level) location, through analytics and advertising services described below.

3. Cookies and advertising

We use third-party advertising (including Google AdSense) to keep this site free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at www.aboutads.info.

4. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate site usage (e.g., which tools are most popular). This data is anonymous and cannot be used to identify you.

5. Third-party links

Our site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other sites and encourage you to read their policies.

6. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at contact@images-resizer.com or visit our contact page.
