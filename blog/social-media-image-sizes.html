Social Media Image Sizes: The Complete 2026 Cheat Sheet

Published August 7, 2026 · Images Resizer Team

Every platform has its own preferred image dimensions, and posting the wrong size means awkward crops, blurry uploads, or wasted space. Bookmark this cheat sheet — it lists the exact pixel sizes that work best on each major platform, and you can resize any image to these dimensions for free in seconds.

Instagram image sizes

TypeSize (px)Aspect ratio
Square post1080 × 10801:1
Portrait post1080 × 13504:5
Landscape post1080 × 5661.91:1
Stories / Reels1080 × 19209:16
Profile picture320 × 3201:1

Tip: Instagram compresses aggressively. Upload at exactly 1080px wide with JPG quality around 85% for the sharpest results — our compressor lets you dial that in.

Pinterest image sizes

TypeSize (px)Aspect ratio
Standard pin1000 × 15002:3
Square pin1000 × 10001:1
Profile picture165 × 1651:1

Pinterest strongly favors the 2:3 vertical format — pins in other ratios get truncated in the feed.

WhatsApp image sizes

TypeSize (px)
Profile picture500 × 500
Status1080 × 1920

WhatsApp heavily compresses shared photos. If quality matters, send the image as a document instead of a photo, and pre-compress it yourself so the file stays under control.

TypeSize (px)

How to hit these sizes exactly

  1. Crop your photo to the right aspect ratio first (1:1, 4:5, 16:9, 9:16 presets are built in).
  2. Then resize it to the exact pixel dimensions from the tables above.
  3. If the platform has a file-size limit, compress to fit.
