Which Format Should You Choose?

JPG is ideal for photographs and images with many colors — small files, universal support. PNG is lossless and supports transparency, making it perfect for logos, screenshots and graphics with text. WebP combines the best of both with even smaller file sizes, ideal for websites.

Popular Image Conversions

PNG to JPG

The most common conversion on the web. Screenshots and graphics saved as PNG are often 5–10× larger than they need to be — converting to JPG at 85–90% quality shrinks them dramatically. Transparent areas are automatically filled with white.

JPG to PNG

Useful when a form or app only accepts PNG uploads, or before editing an image repeatedly — PNG is lossless, so further edits and re-saves won't degrade quality.

WebP to JPG

Images downloaded from modern websites often come as WebP, which some older programs and upload forms don't accept. One click converts them into universally supported JPG.

JPG or PNG to WebP

The best move for website owners: WebP files are 25–35% smaller at equal quality, which speeds up page loads and helps SEO. Convert your site's images to WebP and keep the originals as backups.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert an image to JPG?
Upload your PNG, WebP or GIF image, choose JPG as the output format, and click Convert. The JPG version downloads instantly. Transparent areas are filled with white automatically.
How do I convert JPG to PNG?
Upload the JPG, select PNG as the output format, and click Convert & Download. PNG is lossless, so no further quality is lost after conversion.
What is WebP and should I use it?
WebP is a modern image format by Google that makes files 25–35% smaller than JPG at the same quality. It's supported by all modern browsers and is the best choice for websites.

How do I convert WebP to JPG?
Upload the WebP image, select JPG as the output format and click Convert & Download. This is handy when an older app or website refuses WebP uploads.
Will converting an image change its dimensions?
No — conversion keeps the exact same width and height and only changes how the image is encoded. Use our image resizer if you also want different dimensions.
