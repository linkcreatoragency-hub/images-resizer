Frequently Asked Questions

How do I crop an image online?
Upload your photo, drag the crop box to the area you want to keep, optionally pick an aspect ratio like 1:1 or 16:9, then click Crop & Download. The cropped image saves instantly.
Can I crop an image to a square?
Yes — select the 1:1 aspect ratio button and the crop box will stay perfectly square, ideal for Instagram posts and profile pictures.
Does cropping reduce image quality?
No. Cropping simply removes the outer areas of your photo — the pixels you keep remain at their original quality.
Are my photos uploaded when I crop them?
No. The crop tool runs entirely in your browser, so your images never leave your device.
